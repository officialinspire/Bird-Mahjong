    Field guide

    How to Play

    1. Match pairs. Tap two identical bird tiles to remove them from the board.
    2. Only free tiles move. A tile is free when nothing sits on top of it and its left or right side is open.
    3. Clear the woodland. Remove every tile to win. There's no clock to beat and no way to lose. Take your time.
    4. Need help? Hint highlights a pair you can take right now. Undo puts the last pair back, along with its points. Neither costs anything.
    5. Out of moves? If no pair is left, you'll be offered a Shuffle. It re-deals the birds on the remaining tiles and checks that there's a way to finish. If the tiles left can't be cleared by any deal, Restart Board takes you back to the start of the same board.

    Reading the board

    • Free Bright and raised. You can pick it.
    • Selected Lifted, with a thick ring and a ✓ badge.
    • Blocked Faded, with diagonal stripes. It is covered or boxed in on both sides.
    • Hint A dashed ring and a “?” badge.

    Scoring

    Look closely

    Two dark birds look alike at a glance, but they are different tiles and don't match each other.

    Controls

    Tap or click a tile to select it. When a big board is zoomed in, drag or scroll to pan, and use − / Fit / + to zoom. With a keyboard, Tab moves between free tiles and Enter selects. Escape pauses the game, or goes back a screen elsewhere.
